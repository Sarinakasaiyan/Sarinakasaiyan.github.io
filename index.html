I'm excited to share my research project on machine translation models with you! 🇩🇪🇫🇷

Google Translate

Recently, I conducted a comparative study between T5 and Marian models for translating from English to French and German. This project allowed me to delve deep into the field of natural language processing and evaluate translation quality using the METEOR metric.

Key project highlights:
- Implementation and comparison of T5 and Marian models
- Analysis of translation accuracy and execution time
- Utilization of NLTK and Transformers libraries for natural language processing tasks
- Gaining hands-on experience in tokenization and fine-tuning models

I would like to thank my supervisor, Nava Eslami, for her guidance and support throughout this project. Her expertise in natural language processing has been invaluable to my learning.

For more details about this research project, you can read the article I worked with on Medium: medium.com

I'm eagerly looking forward to continuing my exploration of the fascinating world of natural language processing and machine translation. If you're interested in collaboration or further discussion in this field, I'd be happy to connect with you!

"Line Follower Robot 🚜"

This book, written in the C programming language, helps you enhance your skills in mechanics, electronics, and programming. By studying this book, you can design and implement a line-following robot using an IR sensor and gain a solid understanding of the C programming language and various sensors, especially ultrasonic and IR sensors.

The book includes all the necessary instructions for building a robot capable of moving forward, backward, and turning. All movements and related programming are explained in detail. Additionally, by watching the educational videos, you will better understand the steps to build the robot.

This book is a great opportunity to assess your abilities and identify your interests in robotics and electronics. 💡🤖
